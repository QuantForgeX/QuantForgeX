# ⚡ QuantForgeX

**QuantForgeX** is a trading technology company focused on building **practical, research-driven tools** for systematic trading.

We operate with a simple goal:  
to create software that is **honest in its assumptions, rigorous in its design, and respectful of the user’s time**.

---

## Vision

Our vision is to support the next generation of traders and researchers with tools that:

- Encourage **disciplined research practices**
- Are built with **clarity, correctness, and long-term thinking**

We believe strong systems are formed by confronting constraints early, not hiding them behind abstractions.

---

## Contribution & Collaboration

QuantForgeX is open to collaboration with people who value **research, engineering discipline, and thoughtful problem-solving**.

We welcome:
- Research ideas and methodological discussions  
- Engineering perspectives on trading systems and tooling  
- Constructive feedback on assumptions and design choices  

If you think your experience or ideas align with our direction, we’re open to a conversation.
